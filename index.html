Model. Liquidation is estimated with a strict first-order model: the position is wiped out by an adverse move of 1 ÷ leverage (50x ≈ 2.00%, 100x ≈ 1.00%). Real liquidation prices are usually hit slightly earlier because of maintenance margin, trading fees, funding payments and slippage, and venue-specific liquidation engines can close positions differently.
